The command_translation.help.* entries and vanilla command_translation.tag.*
entries are excerpts, concise summaries and translations of Minecraft Wiki
command and tag articles. They have been
shortened and translated. Their page URLs and exact revision identifiers are
listed in assets/command_translation/documentation/sources.json in this JAR.
The article history links identify the contributing authors.

Other code and translation entries retain their separately stated licenses.
